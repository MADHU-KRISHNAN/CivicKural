## 📋 Walkthrough Overview
**Objective:** Demonstrate full end-to-end automated civic grievance lifecycle:
1. **Multimodal Citizen Reporting:** Text + Audio Voice Notes + GPS Geolocation
2. **AI Engine Processing:** 3-Tier Hierarchy, Cosine Distance Vector Duplicate Detection, EXIF Forensic Trust Scoring, and Dynamic Priority Formula ($0-100$).
3. **Executive Control Center:** GIS Municipal Risk Heatmap, 30-Day Surge Forecast Area Curve, and Triage Dispatch with mandatory completion proof upload.

---

## 🚀 Execution Sequence

### Phase 1: Citizen Submission
1. Navigate to `/report` on Citizen Portal.
2. Select Category (e.g. `Sanitary & Public Hygiene`), capture audio voice note, and attach location photo.
3. System automatically calculates 3-tier taxonomy (`Public Health & Sanitation` -> `Sanitation Board` -> `Garbage & Solid Waste Dump`) and assigns high trust score.

### Phase 2: Executive Triage & Override
1. Log in to `/admin` as Municipal Control Officer (`admin@civickural.gov.in`).
2. Review real-time Leaflet Heatmap and 30-Day Surge Forecast graph.
3. Open `/admin/issues`. Confirm ticket is sorted by `priorityScore` descending.
4. Execute supervisory department override and update status to `Resolved` after providing resolution notes and uploading completion photo proof.
